## Assumptions

- Entirely fictional company, products, prices, volumes, imports and regulation.
- Published HTS examples are not verified classifications for these products; baseline rates are assumptions.
- Annual forecast uses explicit unit customs value × planned annual units, held constant before/after. Historical imports are not annualized or charged retroactively.
- Rate changes are additive percentage points; no exclusions, preference programs, fees or real Chapter 99 stacking are modelled.
- Duty is rounded half-up to cents per SKU per annual scenario. Extra duty = rounded after minus rounded before; totals sum SKU results.

## Method

Match origin AND HTS prefix. Annual customs value = unit customs value × annual planned units. Apply the baseline rate and simulated additive rate to the same value. Round each annual SKU duty to cents, then subtract before from after and sum. No real regulation or actual tariff liability is established.
