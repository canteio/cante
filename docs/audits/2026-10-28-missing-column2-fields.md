# Missing historical Column 2 calculation fields

October 28, 2026 · audit v24 · local acceptance complete; full coverage unfinished.

The known 2017 annual-file defect leaves four calculation fields empty on 548
ordinary source records. The existing compiler now recovers supported formulas
only from complete agreeing annual printed text and exact-code same-year original
schedules. It preserves the empty fields and retains the source/date/page evidence.
It never manufactures a method code, borrows today's rate or creates human approval.

Exact mixed-fraction percentages use the shared rational arithmetic. Square/cubic
unit exponents are rejoined only with unique measured PDF geometry. Known lost
cent glyphs require matching printed originals. Federal-excise-only notes remain
visible and are excluded only from the explicitly customs-duty-only comparison;
other qualifications still block. Equivalent litre spellings match without unit
conversion, preserving original inputs and detecting conflicting quantities.

The PDF reader now recognizes contiguous Chapter 29 endnote pages, separates
published 1.8-point rate baselines and associates raised markers with their nearest
column anchor. It preserves endnotes and ambiguity rather than skipping them.
Multiple/invalid code-key or field-definition originals now produce explicit
definition findings and withhold complete totals. MFN and Column 2 retain separate
field meanings; one selected base column is charged.
