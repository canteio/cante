See the Preface to the tariff schedule for additional information and citations for the Presidential Proclamations referenced herein. Notes : for purposes of this record, the terms “added” and “deleted” refer to the insertion or removal of 8-digit (legal) tariff rate lines or legal notes; the terms “established” or “discontinued” refer to the insertion or removal of 10-digit (nonlegal) statistical reporting numbers or notes. The term “annotated” refers to the creation of new 10 - digit provisions under a previously unsubdivided rate line; the term “restored” refers to the deletion of all annotations under an existing rate line.
