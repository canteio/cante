USITC Tariff Database Fields Item Description HTS8 8-digit HTS number (the legal tariff line) BRIEF_DESCRIPTION Abbreviated product description (maximum of 150 characters and not legally binding or exhaustive) QUANTITY_1_CODE Abbreviation code of first unit of quantity—go to https://dataweb.usitc.gov/tariff/annual and click on “Code Key” link. QUANTITY_2_CODE Abbreviation code of second unit of quantity —go to https://dataweb.usitc.gov/tariff/annual and click on “Code Key” link. WTO_BINDING_CODE B=Bound U=Unbound MFN_TEXT_RATE MFN duty rate in text format MFN_RATE_TYPE_CODE Duty code for tariff calculation—go to https://dataweb.usitc.gov/tariff/annual and click on “Code Key” link. MFN_AVE Not used MFN_AD_VAL_RATE Ad valorem portion of the MFN duty rate (.05 = 5%) MFN_SPECIFIC_RATE Specific portion of the MFN duty rate (.05 = $.05 per unit of quantity) MFN_OTHER_RATE Additional portion of the MFN duty rate (.05 =$.05 per unit of quantity)
