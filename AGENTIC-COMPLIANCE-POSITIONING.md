# Cante: Agent-First Autonomous Compliance ("Compliance Done For You")

---

## 1. The Core Vision & Value Proposition

> **"Your 24/7 autonomous trade compliance team. We watch the laws, audit your shipping documents, and handle customs—so the CEO can sleep peacefully."**

### Why Traditional SaaS Fails in Compliance
* **Traditional SaaS (The Old Way)**: Sells dashboards and search engines. It gives the CEO homework: search tariff tables, read 50-page legal PDFs, and manually audit spreadsheets. Nobody wants to do compliance homework.
* **Cante (The Agent-First Way)**: An autonomous AI workforce running in the background. It does the work for you, proactively resolves bottlenecks, and only asks for 1-click approvals.

## 3. The Target Customer Profile (Who Pays?)

* **Segment**: Mid-Market Physical Goods Companies ($10M – $250M annual revenue).
* **Industries**: Industrial textiles, plastics/packaging converters, machinery assemblers, consumer goods, furniture, chemical formulators.
* **The Buyer Pain**:
  * Cannot afford $800–$1,200/hr trade law firms for daily operations.
  * Customs brokers (CHBs/PPJK) disclaim all legal liability by law.
  * Only 1–2 internal ops managers handling millions of dollars in cross-border cargo.
* **Pricing Model**: $500 – $2,500 / month SaaS subscription.

## 5. The "CEO Relaxes" End-State Workflow

1. **Passive Ingestion**: Invoices and lab certificates are automatically ingested and scanned.
2. **Proactive Mobile Alerts**: If an EPA rule changes or an invoice discrepancy is found, the CEO gets a plain-English message on Telegram/WhatsApp with the exact business impact.
3. **1-Click Execution**: The CEO taps **[Approve & Send]**, and Cante dispatches the email to the foreign factory and the instructions to the customs broker.
